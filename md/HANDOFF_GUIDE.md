# Read-a-Thon Volunteer Handoff Guide

How to run the school Read-a-Thon: who does what, when, the rules, and the school app. Short on purpose - bullets only.

> This guide is public (it ships with the app on GitHub). Names, emails, phone numbers, logins and Google Drive links go in the separate **Contacts & Links** document (Help → Volunteer Handoff Guide → Download Contacts & Links template), kept on the PTA Google Drive.

## At a glance

- **What:** a 10-day reading contest in October. It is the PTA's main fundraiser of the year (it pays for field trips, assemblies, family picnics, the talent show and the Grand Finale).
- **Teams:** every class is on one of **two teams**. The principal and the librarian are the team captains, and a new **theme** (with new team names) is picked each year.
- **Two systems - don't mix them up:**
  - **ReadAThon.com** (the vendor's website): parents register their child, log minutes and collect donations. Volunteers log in there to see reports and **download** the data.
  - **The school Read-a-Thon app** (this app, runs on a volunteer's laptop): we **upload** the ReadAThon.com files. It applies *our* rules (120-minute cap, grade goals, teams, Color War, prizes) and makes the daily and final bulletins.
- **2025 for scale:** ~411 students in 19 classes, about 110,000 minutes read and $31,000 raised.

## Contest rules

These are the rules the school app enforces. If a flyer or slide disagrees, the app is the source of truth - but raise it at the planning meeting.

- **Days:** 10 days in a row, weekends included. Each day counts separately.
- **Logging:** parents log minutes on ReadAThon.com every evening. [TBD: deadline - 2025 said 9 pm the same evening; the 2026 draft letter says by 7 AM the next morning]
- **Cap:** at most **120 minutes a day** count. Reading more is fine but doesn't count.
- **Daily goal by grade** (a student who meets it every day is a **Goal Getter**):

| Grade | Minutes per day | Total over 10 days |
|---|---|---|
| K and 1 | 20 | 200 |
| 2 and 3 | 25 | 250 |
| 4 and 5 | 30 | 300 |

- **Participation:** a student participates on a day when they log more than 0 minutes. Class, grade and team participation = days read ÷ (students × days so far).
- **Color War:** on Color War day, every student wearing their team's colors earns their class and team **+1 participation day and +10 minutes**. A student can get 11 participation days out of 10, so a class can go over 100%.
- **Classes compete as classes:** a half-day kindergarten teacher's AM and PM classes compete separately.
- **Ties:** every tied student or class wins (since 2025; before that, one winner per grade was drawn at random). [TBD: the 2025 slides said a tie for highest class is decided by raffle - the app lists all tied classes. Pick one]
- **Money and sponsors:** totals come from ReadAThon.com.
- **Daily drawing:** each day, two students per grade are drawn from the students who met their daily goal that day (one per grade before 2026; the number is set in the app under Admin → Bulletin Settings). **A student can win only once per contest** (the app leaves out saved winners). If not enough students who met the goal are left who haven't won, the remaining spots go to students who already won.

## Glossary

- **Avg. Participation (with color):** the participation rate the app uses for prizes. Includes Color War points, so it can go over 100%.
- **Bulletins:** the school app's shareable pages (Meet the Teams, Daily Scoreboard, Prize Scoreboard), copied into emails as images.
- **Cap:** the 120-minutes-a-day limit.
- **Captain:** the adult leading a team, usually the principal and the librarian.
- **Class:** one classroom. Half-day kindergarten AM and PM are separate classes with the same teacher.
- **Color War:** a day when students wear their team's colors for bonus participation and minutes.
- **Contest day (Day N):** the Nth day of the contest (Day 1 = kickoff).
- **Cumulative file:** the ReadAThon.com download with each student's totals so far (minutes, money raised, sponsors).
- **Daily drawing:** the random daily prize drawing (see Rules).
- **Daily goal:** the minimum minutes per day for a student's grade (20, 25 or 30).
- **Daily minutes file:** the ReadAThon.com download with each student's minutes for one day.
- **Daily Scoreboard:** the bulletin emailed each morning: team race, top class per grade, drawing winners.
- **Donations / Raised:** money pledged to a student on ReadAThon.com.
- **Editable database:** the one database in the school app that accepts uploads (this year's). Older years are read-only.
- **Final assembly (closing ceremony):** the assembly after the contest where winners are announced.
- **Goal Getter:** a student who met their daily goal every day of the contest.
- **Grade level:** K through 5. Goals and most prizes are per grade.
- **Kickoff assembly:** the assembly on Day 1 that introduces the theme, the teams, their colors and the rules.
- **Meet the Teams:** the bulletin that lists which classes are on which team.
- **Parent letter / take-home packet:** the letter to families about the contest (dates, goals, how to register), with the registration QR code.
- **Participation:** logging more than 0 minutes on a day (see Rules).
- **Planning kickoff meeting:** the volunteers' first planning meeting, months before the contest. Not the kickoff assembly.
- **Prize Scoreboard:** the bulletin with all prize winners; says "Final Prize Winners" after the last day.
- **Read-a-Thon (RAT):** the event itself.
- **ReadAThon.com:** the vendor website used by parents and volunteers.
- **Roster:** the list of students with grade, teacher, class and team.
- **School Read-a-Thon app:** this app. It turns ReadAThon.com downloads into scoreboards and prize winners using our rules.
- **Showdown:** this year compared with last year on the same contest day.
- **Snapshot:** the money and sponsor totals saved for a given contest day (from that day's cumulative file).
- **Sponsor:** a person who pledged money to a student.
- **Student / Reader:** a child in the roster ("Reader" on ReadAThon.com).
- **Teacher:** a class's teacher. One teacher can have two classes (AM and PM kindergarten).
- **Team:** one of the two sides; every class belongs to one.
- **Theme:** the year's story (e.g. Willy Wonka in 2025). Team names, decorations and prize names follow it.
- **Top Student Earner:** the student who raised the most money on each team.

## The school app

Runs on a laptop at http://127.0.0.1:5001. **✨ Simple view** (top right) shows only what the daily job needs: Upload, Bulletins, Help. More detail in Help → User Manual.

### Before the contest

- **Admin → Database Registry → Create New Database:** this year's year plus three files from Appendix A (roster, class info, grade rules). It becomes the editable database.
- **Admin → Actions → Bulletin Settings:** school name and number of contest days (10).
- **Bulletins → Meet the Teams:** Copy as image → email to the school.

### Every morning (Day 1 to Day 10, plus the morning after the last day)

1. **ReadAThon.com:** download yesterday's **daily minutes** file and the **cumulative** file. [TBD: exact ReadAThon.com menu path and report names]
2. **Upload → Daily Minutes Upload:** choose the day's file.
3. **Upload → Cumulative Stats Upload:** check the **snapshot date** = the contest day the file covers.
4. **Bulletins → Daily Scoreboard:** check the day, then **Save winners** (the drawing is final once saved).
5. **Copy as image** → paste into the email to the school (or Download PNG and attach it).

### Color War day

- **Upload → Team Color Bonus Upload:** the date plus a file with one row per class (columns: timestamp, class_name, team_name, grade_level, students_count).

### After the contest

- **Bulletins → Prize Scoreboard:** Final Prize Winners, for the final assembly slides.
- **Reports & Data:** extra stats for the slides (e.g. students who read every day).

## Installing and running the app

The tech-team lead can help with the first install. Real student data is **not** on GitHub - only the code is.

- **Computer:** a Mac for now. [TBD: Windows version]
- **Get the code:** https://github.com/stevensouza/readathon → **Code → Download ZIP** (unzip it), or `git clone https://github.com/stevensouza/readathon.git`.
- **Install (one time):** open Terminal in the folder and run `./install.sh`. It installs what's missing (it may ask for your Mac password) and puts **Start Read-a-Thon** and **Stop Read-a-Thon** shortcuts on the Desktop. If it says "permission denied", run `chmod +x install.sh` first.
- **Start / stop:** double-click the Desktop shortcuts. The browser opens the app.
- **Get the data:** the previous volunteer stops the app, runs `./package_data.sh` and shares the zip **privately** (it has student names). Unzip it inside the app folder with `unzip -o <zip file>`.
- **Update:** download the new ZIP (or `git pull`), copy over the `db` folder if needed, then run `./install.sh` again.
- **More:** Help → Installation Guide.

## Appendix A: Roster from the school

Ask the secretary/principal for **one spreadsheet (Excel or Google Sheets), one row per student**, with these columns:

| Column | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| First_Name | Avery | as the family will register the child |
| Last_Name | Smith | |
| Grade | K | K, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (not 0 for kindergarten) |
| Teacher | Smith | teacher's last name |
| Session | AM | half-day kindergarten only: AM or PM; blank for everyone else |
| Home_Room | KA-16 | |

2025's roster came as Last_Name, First_Name, Grade_Level (0 = K), Home_Room, Teacher. If the school can't use the format above, that's fine - convert it with the prompt below.

**The app needs three CSV files** (they contain student names, so never put them on GitHub):

- `roster.csv`: student_name, class_name, home_room, teacher_name, grade_level, team_name
- `class_info.csv`: class_name, home_room, teacher_name, grade_level, team_name, total_students
- `grade_rules.csv`: grade_level, min_daily_minutes, max_daily_minutes_credit (one row per grade, e.g. `K,20,120` ... `5,30,120`)

**AI prompt to convert the school's roster** (paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or similar together with the spreadsheet):

```
I am attaching a school roster spreadsheet. Convert it into two CSV files for our Read-a-Thon app.

1. roster.csv with the header: student_name,class_name,home_room,teacher_name,grade_level,team_name
   - student_name: "First Last" (first name, one space, last name), exactly as spelled in the roster
   - teacher_name: the teacher's last name in all lowercase (e.g. "smith", "o'neil")
   - class_name: same as teacher_name, except half-day kindergarten classes: "<teacher_name> am" or "<teacher_name> pm"
   - home_room: as given
   - grade_level: K, 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 (convert 0 to K)
   - team_name: leave empty for now
2. class_info.csv with the header: class_name,home_room,teacher_name,grade_level,team_name,total_students
   - one row per class_name; total_students = number of students in that class; team_name empty for now

Then list anything you were unsure about (missing grades, a teacher with two classes, duplicate names, blank cells).
Give me both files as downloadable CSV files.
```

## Appendix B: Making the teams

In 2025 the teams were made with AI from the roster and a list of rules. The team names come from the theme. After the school OKs the teams, fill in `team_name` in both CSV files and create the database.

**Rules used so far** (the special requests themselves are private - keep them in the Contacts doc):

- Two teams, with student counts as close as possible.
- A teacher's AM and PM kindergarten classes are on the same team.
- Each team has at least one class from every grade.
- Special requests, e.g. the principal asked for certain classes to be on opposite teams, and a parent asked for their children to be on opposite teams.

**AI prompt to make the teams** (attach `class_info.csv` and, for sibling requests, `roster.csv`):

```
I am attaching class_info.csv (one row per class with total_students) for our school Read-a-Thon.
Split the classes into two teams named "<Team 1 name>" and "<Team 2 name>".

Rules:
1. The two teams' total student counts must be as close as possible.
2. Classes with the same teacher_name (half-day kindergarten AM and PM) must be on the same team.
3. Each team must have at least one class from every grade (K, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
4. These classes must be on opposite teams: <list of class_name pairs>
5. These students must be on opposite teams: <list of student names>. Put their classes on opposite teams.

Give me:
- a table of classes by team with the student counts and the team totals
- class_info.csv and roster.csv with team_name filled in, as downloadable CSV files
- a check of every rule above (pass or fail)
```
